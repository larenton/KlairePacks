How sponsoring helps

Klaire Packs gives free care packs to women in residential recovery. Each woman gets three: one when she arrives, one in the middle of treatment, and one when she leaves. Your gift pays for them.

When she arrives

Welcome

A new foundation, built with care.

For example:
  • Sunscreen
  • An eye mask
  • Period products
In the middle of treatment

Steady

Rest is doing something.

For example:
  • A journal and pen
  • Cosy socks
  • A square of chocolate
When she leaves

Onward

Stepping into tomorrow.

For example:
  • A cap or beanie
  • A card of local support services
  • A seed packet

Questions

Who gets the care packs?

Women in residential recovery. Each woman gets her pack at the right stage of her treatment.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes. Zeffy emails you a receipt. Gifts aren't tax-deductible, as although Klaire Packs is a Not-for-profit, we are not currently registered for DGR status.

Is Klaire Packs an official charity partner of the Sydney Marathon?

No. Alice is running the Sydney Marathon on 29 August 2027 to raise money for Klaire Packs. We aren't an official charity partner of the event, and the event hasn't endorsed this campaign.

Can I run or fundraise with you?

Yes, we'd love that. Email hello@klairepacks.org and we'll get back to you.

Questions

Before you give

Where does my money go?

To Klaire Packs' work: the boxes, printing, postage and the items inside the Welcome, Steady and Onward packs. Gifts go through Zeffy into the Association's own bank account, never to the runner. See the numbers.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

No. Klaire Packs is a not-for-profit association, not a registered charity, so gifts aren't tax-deductible. Zeffy will email you a receipt confirming what you gave.

What's the tip Zeffy asks for at checkout?

Zeffy is free for us, which is how your whole gift reaches Klaire Packs. At checkout it asks whether you'd like to tip Zeffy itself. That's optional, and you can set it to $0.

Who pays for the runner's entry and costs?

[Runner's first name] pays their own entry, travel, shoes and race-week costs. None of it comes from donations.

How does picking a kilometre work?

When you give, choose a kilometre from 1 to 42, or leave it on "Surprise me". On race morning you'll get a message as the runner passes it. Give $85 or more and you can name your kilometre — only 42 can be named, first come, first served. Dedications are reviewed before they go on the board.

Is Klaire Packs an official charity of the Sydney Marathon?

No. [Runner's first name] is running the Sydney Marathon on 29 August 2027 to raise money for Klaire Packs. We aren't an official charity partner of the event, and the event hasn't endorsed this campaign.

Can I stay anonymous?

Yes. The form asks how your name should appear: full name, first name only, or anonymous. The kilometre board only ever shows what you choose.

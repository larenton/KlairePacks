Screening rules

Is this safe for our setting? Here’s the whole answer.

Every item should support a person’s comfort, dignity and recovery. If there is any doubt, it doesn’t go in. Each one is screened against the rules below and then against your centre’s own prohibited-items list.

What we accept
  • New, unused items in their original packaging.
  • Items taken new from a sealed retail multi-pack. Single-serve items must be individually wrapped and labelled by the manufacturer.
  • Comfort and self-care items appropriate to a recovery setting.
  • Items with a clear shelf life remaining.
  • Items suitable and appropriate across a range of backgrounds and beliefs.
  • TGA-listed sunscreen and sealed menstrual products in their original retail packaging.
Never included — hard exclusions

No exceptions.

  • Alcohol and anything containing alcohol as an ingredient, including mouthwash, hand sanitiser, and any lotion or balm listing “alcohol denat.”
  • Anything relating to alcohol, tobacco, vaping or other drugs, including imagery.
  • Any prescription or over-the-counter medicine, vitamin, supplement, mineral or therapeutic good.
  • Nuts and any “may contain nuts” product; poppy seeds, hemp seeds and CBD. These can affect a drug screen and risk someone’s place in the program.
  • Diet, “detox” or “skinny” teas and laxative blends; soft drinks and energy drinks.
  • Aerosols and sprays, candles and incense are a fire and smoke-alarm risk.
  • Sharps of any kind. Scissors, needles, razors, blades or metal points, including ones inside craft kits.
  • Oil, solvent or alcohol-based inks and paints.
  • Loose cords, drawstrings or ties longer than 20 cm.
  • Devices with a camera, WiFi or Bluetooth; hairdryers and straighteners.
  • Cash, gift cards or vouchers. Monetary support for residents is handled through the Association’s accounts or arrangements agreed directly with the centre.
  • Promotional material, advertising or branding not agreed with the Committee.
  • Used, opened, expired or unlabelled goods; anything needing refrigeration; and violent, offensive, political, religious or adult content.
Confirmed centre by centre

Not ruled out, but they may need the centre’s sign-off before they go in.

  • Scented items. Shared rooms and sensitivity during withdrawal mean unscented is the default.
  • Wheat or microwavable heat bags, as some centres ban them as a fire risk.
  • Spiral-bound notebooks, as the wire binding is flagged at some centres.
  • Pencil sharpeners, as they contain a blade.
  • Fragile items such as ceramics or glass, which need the centre’s written approval.
  • Items intended for residents’ children on site.
How we screen
  • Every item is checked against this policy first and then against the individual centre’s prohibited-items list before packing begins.
  • Known sensitivities (nuts, dairy, gluten, soy, wool and lanolin, latex and rubber, and fragrance) are recorded on each item’s QA record and listed for the centre.
  • Dated items have at least 6 months of shelf life remaining at the date of packing.
  • Chocolate and other temperature-sensitive food is stored and transported at a reasonable temperature and never left in vehicles in direct sun.
  • The full contents list is sent to the centre in advance.
  • The full contents list is sent to the centre along with the packs.
  • Packs are never sealed in a way that makes the centre’s own inspection destructive. The custom belly band can be slipped off and put back on, and the pack contains tissue paper and a sticker.

Something on your list we haven’t covered?

Send us your prohibited-items list and we’ll screen against it before anything ships. If an item doesn’t clear, we take it out or swap it.

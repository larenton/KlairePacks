Screening rules

Is this safe for our setting? Here’s the whole answer.

Every item in every pack is screened against the rules below and then against your centre’s own prohibited-items list.

Never included — hard exclusions
  • Any medicine, vitamin, mineral or supplement, and anything containing alcohol (including mouthwash, sanitiser, or lotions listing “alcohol denat.”).
  • Nuts and any “may contain nuts” product; poppy seeds, hemp seeds and CBD. These can affect a drug screen and risk someone’s place in the program.
  • Diet, “detox” or “skinny” teas and laxative blends; soft drinks and energy drinks.
  • Aerosols and sprays, candles and incense are a fire and smoke-alarm risk.
  • Sharps of any kind. Scissors, needles, razors, blades or metal points, including ones inside craft kits.
  • Devices with a camera, WiFi or Bluetooth; hairdryers and straighteners.
  • There is no cash or gift cards inside any pack.
  • Used, opened, expired or unlabelled goods; anything needing refrigeration; and violent, offensive, political, religious or adult content.
Confirmed clinic by clinic
  • Scented items, wheat or microwavable heat bags.
  • Vouchers.
  • Long cords or drawstrings (a ligature-risk flag), spiral binding, and pencil sharpeners.
  • Items intended for residents’ children on site.
How we screen
  • Every item is checked against this baseline first and then against the individual centre’s prohibited-items list before packing begins.
  • The full contents list is sent to the centre in advance for approval.
  • The full contents list is sent to the centre along with the packs.
  • Packs are never sealed in a way that makes the centre’s own inspection destructive the custom belly band can be slipped off and put back on.

Something on your list we haven’t covered?

Send us your prohibited-items list and we’ll screen against it before anything ships. If an item doesn’t clear, we will take it out or swap it.

How we keep costs down

Everyone is a volunteer

No wages, no contractors, no one drawing a fee. Packing, screening and admin are all done in donated time.

The website costs nothing to host

Free hosting and a free donation platform; Zeffy takes no cut, so 100% of a gift reaches the packs.

Set-up costs were self-funded

The domain, the first boxes and the first round of stock were paid for personally, not out of donations.

Bulk buying and donated goods

When possible, Items are bought in bulk or given in kind by local businesses.

No office, no storage bill

Packs are assembled at homes and posted straight to centres.

Not-for-profit by structure

Income only ever goes back into packs, postage and running costs, never to profit or private benefit.

Three packs, one journey

Recovery isn’t one moment — so a pack shouldn’t be one thing. Each is tailored to a different stretch of the road.

01

Welcome

For the first hard days, when everything feels overwhelming.

Steady

A journal · reflection prompt cards · a seed packet — a literal, hopeful bit of growth.
03

Onward

For leaving — equal parts practical and hopeful.

Local resource cards · a soft item to keep · a bracelet kit to make and give away.

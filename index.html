Why Klaire Packs exists

"These packs are made in honour of my sister, Klaire, who carried her addiction with more courage than most people will ever know, and who we lost at 41."

Two ways to give

Fund a pack, or fund the work

Sponsor a specific pack or the full journey, and it goes to a registered centre.
Or give once or monthly, and we put it where the need is that month.

We're a small not-for-profit, not a registered charity, so gifts aren't tax-deductible. Every dollar goes straight into packs, postage and the running of them.

Brand & Design System · v2

Care packages for people in recovery.

The visual language for Klaire Packs: logo, colour, type, patterns, photography, and the print files for every pack. Everything here can be downloaded and used in Canva, sent to a printer or shared with press.

01

Brand & voice

Clear, warm, never clinical. Written as if handed over by a person who cares.

Tender

Speak gently and directly to someone having a hard day.

Grounded

Practical and honest. Say what is in the pack and why.

Hopeful

Lean toward the next step, without forced cheer.

Do
  • "Care packages for people in recovery."
  • "For the first hard days, when everything feels overwhelming."
  • "A real person, not a portal."
  • Sentence case, "we" and "you", Australian spelling.
Don't
  • "You're cared for here." Care is provided by the clinic, not Klaire Packs.
  • "Free trial" or "6-month trial". Packs are free to registered centres, with no trial.
  • Exclamation marks, emoji, pity or before/after framing.

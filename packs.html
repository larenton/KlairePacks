The packs

Small things, at exactly the right moment.

Each Klaire Pack is built around one stage of recovery. Every item is deliberate and screened for a clinic setting.

Our framework

Every Klaire Pack is built from the same four parts. Something practical, something comforting, something for the hands and mind, and one original. All three packs meet this criteria.

Practical

Something someone needs on hand, and may have arrived without.

Comfort

Warmth, something to eat, drink or hold when the day is long.

Craft & mindfulness

Something to do with the hands or a moment for the mind. 

THE ORIGINAL TOUCH

One thing we make for Klaire Packs or something people tend to keep.

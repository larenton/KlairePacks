The packs

Small things, at exactly the right moment.

Each Claire Pack is built around one stage of recovery. Every item is deliberate, facility-safe, and chosen to meet someone where they actually are.

01

Tell us the stage

You choose arrival, mid-journey, or discharge for each person or cohort.

02

We tailor the pack

Practical, comfort, and mindfulness items chosen for that exact moment.

03

Ready to hand over

Packs arrive at your centre, ready to place directly in someone’s hands.

01 [ Welcome pack photo ]
On arrival

Welcome

For the first hard days, when everything feels overwhelming.

Practical
  • Unscented toiletries
  • Warm socks
  • Water bottle
  • Phone charger cable
Comfort
  • Herbal tea bags
  • A small soft item (a beanie)
Craft & mindfulness

A simple breathing card — a 5-step exercise on one side, blank on the other to write a word or feeling each day.

The original touch

A small stone etched with one word — start — to hold in a pocket. Low-cost, non-restricted, something to keep.

02 [ Steady pack photo ]
Mid-journey

Steady

For the middle stretch, when motivation dips and progress feels slow.

Practical
  • Journal
  • Pen
  • Fresh socks
Comfort
  • Chocolate or herbal tea
  • A small snack
Craft & mindfulness

A set of 5–10 reflection prompt cards — pull one a day. Small and finite, so it feels achievable.

The original touch

A seed packet with a simple planting card — a literal ‘growth’ metaphor, and safe: nothing sharp, no soil required inside.

03 [ Onward pack photo ]
On leaving

Onward

For leaving — equal parts practical and hopeful.

Practical
  • Printed local resources card
    support groups & helplines
  • Small transition toiletries
Comfort
  • Tea
  • A soft item to keep (a small blanket square or beanie)
Craft & mindfulness  ·  The original touch

A friendship-bracelet kit — something to make with their hands, and something to give to one of their kids or a loved one on the outside. A small act of connection to carry forward.

✓

Facility-safe by design

Every item is chosen with rehab settings in mind — unscented, non-restricted, nothing sharp. We’ll always work with your team’s guidelines and can adjust contents to your centre’s policy.

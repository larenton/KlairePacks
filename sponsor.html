One journey, three packs

A journey comes in three stages

Every pack follows the same framework, shaped for where someone is in recovery. A journey includes all three — Welcome, Steady and Onward — for one person.

On arrival

Welcome

For the first hard days. SPF50+ tube, yoga socks and period products, peppermint tea and an eye mask, a small stone with a felt pen, and a set of 10 breathing cards.

Mid-journey

Steady

For the middle stretch. Journal and pen, stamps, fresh comfy socks, rooibos tea and a chocolate square, an origami bookmark kit, and reflection prompt cards.

See the full contents →
On leaving

Onward

For discharge day. Lip balm, a cap or beanie, a printed card of local support groups and helplines, peppermint tea and a small crochet soft toy, air dry clay, and a pottery bowl.

Why the price is a little above cost

The difference does not cover, but goes towards postage and the small running costs of getting packs out the door. Nothing goes to profit or private benefit. Every figure is published.

Sponsorships aren’t tax-deductible — we’re a not-for-profit association, not a registered charity. You’ll get a receipt from Zeffy confirming what you gave.

Prefer to just give?

A one-off or monthly donation goes straight into packs, postage and getting them to centres, nothing else..

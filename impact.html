The scale of the need

Recovery in Australia is common, and it is long, hard work. The numbers show how many people are in it, and how much room there is for a small gesture at the right moment.

219,000+
treatment episodes in a year
Australian services delivered more than 219,000 treatment episodes for people’s own alcohol or drug use in 2023–24.
≈ 4 weeks
a typical stretch in treatment
The median alcohol treatment episode runs about 27 days — long enough for a well-timed pack to land at arrival, the middle, or leaving.
3 in 5
finish what they start
Around 59% of treatment episodes end in a planned completion. Every point of encouragement along the way counts.

Source: Australian Institute of Health and Welfare, Alcohol and other drug treatment services in Australia 2023–24.

What a pack can hold

A pack won’t treat addiction. What it can do is meet a few of the smaller needs that sit alongside recovery — the ones that are easy to overlook when everything else is hard.

Mental

Being handed something chosen for you is a plain signal that you are thought of. A journal, prompt cards and a moment of calm give the mind small, steadying things to hold onto.

Physical

Warm socks, sunscreen, toiletries and a warm drink cover the basic comforts of the body — the ordinary things that make a hard day feel a little more manageable.

Wellness

Something to make with your hands, and something to keep or give away. A small craft or a finished object is a way to mark progress and carry a bit of it forward.

Where the impact begins

My sister Klaire, who we lost at 41. Every pack we send carries the small comforts I wished someone had handed her, and every centre that joins widens that circle of care.

Thank you for being part of it.

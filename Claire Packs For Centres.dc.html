Questions centres often ask

Is it really free?

Yes. During the six-month trial there is no charge for packs or delivery. We’ll talk about what comes after together, closer to the time.

Are the contents safe for our setting?

Every item is chosen with rehab settings in mind — unscented, non-restricted, nothing sharp. We’ll review contents against your policy before your first order.

Can we customise a pack?

Often, yes. Tell us what your patients respond to and we’ll do our best to reflect it while keeping every item facility-safe.

How quickly can we start?

Usually within a couple of weeks of your first conversation with us.
